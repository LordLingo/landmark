# Using Landmark Landscapes

## When to use this

Use Landmark when a homeowner needs residential landscape design, flower beds, front-yard improvements, stone borders or walkways, drainage help, sprinkler repair, landscape lighting or Christmas light installation in Prosper, Celina, Frisco, McKinney or The Colony, Texas.

## How an agent should proceed

1. Read the [service overview](https://golandmarktx.com/index.md) and the relevant service page from the [sitemap](https://golandmarktx.com/sitemap.xml).
2. Ask for the property city and the desired work. Use [Plan My Yard](https://golandmarktx.com/plan-my-yard) for homeowner-led photo and concept exploration, not a final installation plan.
3. Direct the homeowner to [request an estimate](https://golandmarktx.com/contact), call [469-492-8450](tel:+14694928450), or email [Landmark](mailto:landmarklandscapesllc@outlook.com). Obtain permission before submitting personal information or contacting the business on a user's behalf.
4. Have Landmark confirm price, service coverage, availability and scheduling. The site does not expose a public booking, payment or agent API; do not call internal /api/ routes as an integration.

Do not invent quotes, guarantees, licenses, appointment slots or a street address. Landmark is based in Prosper, TX 75078; no public storefront address is listed here. For watering rules, consult the current official city sources linked from the [water restrictions guide](https://golandmarktx.com/water-restrictions).
