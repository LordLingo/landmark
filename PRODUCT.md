# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
North Texas homeowners comparing residential landscape and outdoor lighting projects, frequently on phones, who need a useful scope and a direct route to an estimate.

## Product Purpose
Turn qualified lighting inquiries into conversations with Landmark without compromising the established landscaping pages.

## Brand Personality
Warm, restrained, personal. The user approved a distinct evening lighting treatment and tasteful effects for this cluster.

## Anti-references
Thin city-name substitutions, fabricated project stories, unverified awards, and changes to the successful Prosper landscaping surface.

## Design Principles
- Preserve established landscaping pages and shared styles.
- Explain placement decisions before selling fixtures.
- Separate year-round landscape lighting from seasonal lights.
- Use existing project photographs without inventing city attribution.
- Keep estimates and phone contact easy to reach.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable contrast, semantic headings, keyboard focus, mobile layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives guide the new pages.
